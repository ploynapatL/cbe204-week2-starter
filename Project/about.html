Our Story

Born from a rainy afternoon.

Rainshine Books began with a simple question: what if getting a great book felt as comforting as finding your favorite chair?

So we built a little rental library for people who love stories but don't always want to buy another book. Our collection stays intentionally small, our service stays human, and our prices stay gentle.

Questions, answered

FAQ

How does book rental pricing work?

Each book has a base rental price shown on its card. Longer rental durations are priced at 1× for 7 days, 1.45× for 14 days, and 1.9× for 30 days.

Can I rent more than one book?

Absolutely. Add as many stories as you'd like to your basket; the service fee is calculated once per rental order.

Do I pay online?

No. This website is a front-end demo and does not collect payments or create accounts.

Can I choose pickup or delivery?

Yes. Select your preference in the rental confirmation form. In a real Rainshine shop, that choice would be used to arrange your order.

What happens if I need the book longer?

For this demo, choose a longer duration directly in your basket. In a real rental, we'd simply help you extend it if the book isn't reserved.
